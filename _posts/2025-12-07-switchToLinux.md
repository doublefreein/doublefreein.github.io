The poster above in plain text:

## Why Linux Is the Smart Choice

1. **Long-Term Cost Savings** — no license fees, works efficiently on older
   hardware, reduces upgrade cycles (hardware lasts 2× longer), free
   open-source productivity tools included.
2. **Performance & Reliability** — fast, secure, and stable; immune to most
   viruses; automatic updates without forced reboots; excellent for labs and
   shared systems.
3. **Perfect for Schools & Learning** — helps develop real computing skills;
   used by global tech leaders (Google, NASA, Red Hat); encourages
   creativity, programming, and STEM; strong developer ecosystem.
4. **Empowers a Local Tech Community** — opens opportunities in cloud,
   DevOps, and cybersecurity; builds local Linux talent in India; encourages
   open-source contributions; reduces dependency on expensive vendors.

## Cost Comparison: Linux vs Windows vs macOS

For schools and small home offices in India — includes installation and
annual maintenance.

|                        | Linux                             | Windows                  | macOS                                  |
|------------------------|-----------------------------------|--------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| OS License Cost        | ₹0 (open source)                  | ₹6,000–₹12,000 per device | Included with Apple hardware (devices cost 3×) |
| Installation Cost      | ₹1,000–₹1,500                     | ₹1,500–₹2,500            | ₹2,000–₹3,500                          |
| Annual Maintenance     | ₹3,000–₹6,000                     | ₹6,000–₹12,000           | ₹7,000–₹15,000                         |
| Hardware Requirements  | Low-spec, older PCs               | Mid/high-spec            | Apple hardware                         |
| Virus/Antivirus Cost   | None needed                       | ₹500–₹1,500 yearly       | Usually not needed                     |
| Software Cost          | Mostly free (LibreOffice, GIMP)   | Paid (MS Office, antivirus) | Paid (Office, creative tools)       |
| 5-Year TCO (per PC)    | **₹15,000–₹25,000**               | ₹35,000–₹70,000          | ₹1,00,000+ (hardware included)         |

**Linux is 40%–70% cheaper over 5 years** — perfect for computer labs,
libraries, staff rooms, tuition centers, and small offices.

## Sustainable & Community-Focused

- Extends the life of old systems → less e-waste
- Encourages local open-source groups and student clubs
- Builds digital independence and self-reliance
- Skills learned on Linux are a foundation for IT careers
